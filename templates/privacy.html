Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the app, such as pages visited and actions taken, used solely to improve the Service.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, or any information beyond what is necessary to provide the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain your personal expense tracking experience.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Improve the reliability and usability of the Service.
  • Respond to support requests you initiate.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than delivering the Service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms before being saved. Expense data is associated only with your account and is not accessible to other users.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own use. We may use limited third-party infrastructure services (such as hosting providers) to operate the Service. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for any purpose beyond supporting our Service.

We do not integrate with third-party analytics platforms or advertising networks.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided within the app. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly.
